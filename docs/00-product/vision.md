# Bright OS Vision

## Mission

Bright OS is an operations platform for preschool education.

The goal is not to become another CRM.

The goal is to replace the operational Google Workspace used by Bright Preschool and automate repetitive administrative work.

## Core Principles

1. Workflow over modules.
2. One source of truth.
3. Every entity belongs to a Branch.
4. Automate repetitive work, not decisions.
5. Preserve business logic from existing spreadsheets.
6. Cash-based management reporting.
7. Search-first experience.
8. Simplicity before features.

## Current Scope

Version 1 focuses on replacing:

- Google Forms
- Google Sheets
- Manual contract creation
- Manual invoice generation
- Manual financial tracking

Future versions may include:

- Parent portal
- Teacher portal
- Attendance
- AI assistant
- Mobile application
